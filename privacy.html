Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026. This policy covers Leadzen CRM and Leadzen Connect, the lead-management tools that Leadzen Marketing (Bengaluru, India) runs for its advertising clients.

Who decides and who processes. Each client business decides why and how its leads are used; under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 the client is the Data Fiduciary. Leadzen Marketing processes that data only on the client's instructions, as its Data Processor.

What we collect. When a person fills a lead form for one of our clients (a Meta / Facebook / Instagram Lead Ad, a website form, or a sheet the client shares with us), we receive the answers they gave: usually name, phone number, email, city and the questions asked in that form, plus the campaign, ad and form the lead came from. The client's team then adds notes, call outcomes, follow-up dates and deal details in the CRM. We do not collect payment details.

How we use it. Only to deliver each lead to the client's own CRM workspace, help the client's sales team follow it up, and report campaign results (for example, cost per lead) to that client. We do not sell personal data, do not use one client's leads for another client, and do not use lead data for our own advertising.

Meta Platform data. Leads from Meta Lead Ads are fetched with the advertiser's permission through the Meta Marketing API (leads_retrieval) and stored only in that advertiser's workspace. Meta data is used only for the purposes above and is deleted as described below.

Who can see it. Only users the client approves in its own workspace, and Leadzen Marketing staff who run the service. Each client's data sits in a separate workspace. Data is stored on servers in India, access is password protected, and backups are kept private.

How long we keep it. For as long as the client uses the service. Within 30 days after a client leaves, its workspace and backups are deleted, unless the client asks us to export the data first or the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting the business whose form you filled or by writing to us (below). We will pass your request to that business and act on it.

Data deletion

To have your data deleted from Leadzen CRM:

  1. Email marketingleadzen@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the phone number and/or email address you used in the form, and the business name if you know it.
  3. We confirm receipt within 3 working days and delete your lead record, its notes and its activity history from the relevant workspace within 30 days. Backups holding it expire within a further 30 days.
  4. We reply to confirm the deletion.

You can also remove Leadzen's access to your Facebook data at any time from Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

Contact / grievance officer: Leadzen Marketing, Bengaluru, India — marketingleadzen@gmail.com.
